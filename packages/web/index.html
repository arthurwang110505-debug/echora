ECHORA 沉浸式動態歌詞音樂舞台 · Immersive synced-lyrics stage

Echora 是一款在瀏覽器運行的沉浸式歌詞與音樂舞台。播放內建展示曲目，或連接自己的 YouTube Music 歌單，就能看到逐字同步歌詞、視覺舞台與 AI 生成配色。Echora 沒有自己的帳號系統，也不會把你的歌單或聽歌紀錄上傳到我們的伺服器。

Echora is a browser-based immersive lyrics and music stage. Play the built-in demo tracks, or connect your own YouTube Music playlists for word-by-word lyrics, visualizer stages and AI-generated themes. Echora has no account system of its own and never uploads your playlists or listening history to our servers.
